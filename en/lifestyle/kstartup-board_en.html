Why this board exists

South Korea runs a large number of government startup funding programs, and new calls appear every week on the national K-Startup portal. Scanning them means opening notice after notice, since a title alone rarely tells you who is eligible, which region it covers, or when it closes.

This page lays out one week of announcements as cards. Category, region and deadline sit on the card itself, so you can skim rather than click through, and open only what looks relevant.

A note on language

The listings are in Korean and stay that way. These programs are administered by Korean government bodies and their official notices are published only in Korean. Programme names and eligibility wording carry legal weight, so translating them here would risk changing what they actually say. The interface, this guide and the search filters are in English; the announcement text is left exactly as issued.

If you need a rough reading, your browser's built-in translation will handle the cards well enough to decide what to open. For anything you intend to apply to, read the original notice.

How to use it

  1. The page loads this week's announcements, 15 cards per page.
  2. Search by keyword, or narrow the list with the category, region and priority filters.
  3. Switch the sort to closing soonest to surface urgent deadlines.
  4. Click any card to open the original K-Startup notice in a new tab.

Reading the cards

Coloured bar on the left
A personal priority marking added after reviewing the announcements, mirrored in the colour of the programme title. A stronger colour means it is worth a closer look. This is not an official rating.
D-number next to the deadline
Days remaining, shown only when a deadline falls within two weeks. Past deadlines are marked as closed (마감).
Eligibility line
The plain black line in the middle of each card, stating who may apply — company stage, sector or size as written in the original notice.
Funding line
The navy bold box beneath it, showing what is actually on offer. It is set apart because the amount is usually the first thing anyone checks.
공고문 확인
Literally "check the announcement". It appears when the source notice carries no summary of what is offered — open the card for the details.
Empty fields
Where the source gives no region or funding summary, the badge is simply left off rather than filled with a placeholder.

Frequently asked questions

Why are the listings in Korean?
These are South Korean government funding programs, and the official announcements on K-Startup are published only in Korean. Translating programme names or eligibility wording would risk changing their meaning, so the listings are kept exactly as issued. The interface and this guide are in English.
Can foreign founders apply to these programs?
It depends on the programme. Some are open to foreign nationals holding a Korean business registration or a startup visa, while many require Korean residency or a locally incorporated company. Eligibility is stated in each announcement, so open the original notice and check before applying.
How often is the list updated?
Weekly. The header shows which year, month and week the data covers, along with the time it was loaded.
What do the coloured bars on the left of each card mean?
They are a personal priority marking added after reviewing the announcements. A stronger colour, matched by the programme title, means the item is worth a closer look. It is not an official rating.
Where does clicking a card take me?
It opens the original announcement on the K-Startup portal in a new tab. Applications are submitted there, not on this page.

This board is a reference compiled from published announcements. Eligibility, deadlines and required documents must be confirmed in the original K-Startup notice — details can be missed in compilation, and notices are sometimes revised after publication.
