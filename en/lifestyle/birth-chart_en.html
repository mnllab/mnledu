Two people born on the same day do not share a chart

If you only look at which sign the Sun was in, everyone born in the same month arrives at the same conclusion. But nine other bodies were also somewhere specific in that sky, and their arrangement changes with the time and place of birth.

The Ascendant in particular — the point rising on the eastern horizon — moves about one degree every four minutes. Two people born in the same city two hours apart have Ascendants nearly thirty degrees apart, which shifts every house cusp and rearranges where the planets fall.

This tool reconstructs that difference through real astronomical calculation. No lookup table maps a birth date to a sign; the coordinates are computed for the moment itself.

What it calculates

Ecliptic longitudes of ten bodies
Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto, computed as geocentric coordinates through Astronomy Engine and shown to the arcminute.
Ascendant and Midheaven
Derived from local sidereal time, the latitude of the birth place, and the obliquity of the ecliptic at that date — giving the point where the ecliptic meets the eastern horizon, and the point where it crosses the meridian.
The twelve houses
Equal House system, taking the Ascendant as the cusp of the 1st House and dividing thirty degrees at a time. Each body is then assigned to the house it falls in.
Major aspects
Conjunction, sextile, square, trine and opposition, found within their allowed orbs and reported with the actual orb.
Element and modality distribution
How the bodies scatter across Fire, Earth, Air and Water, and across Cardinal, Fixed and Mutable, shown as proportions.

About birth time and time zones

The time you enter is the local civil time at the birth place — the time the clock actually showed. There is no need to convert it to UTC.

Selecting a city applies that region’s IANA time zone, and the real UTC offset and daylight-saving state for your birth date are worked out from it. New York is never fixed at UTC−5, so a July 1990 birth correctly uses UTC−4.

If you enter a time that did not exist because of a spring-forward transition, the form tells you. When the time sits close to a fall-back window where the same hour occurs twice, it suggests verifying the record.

Frequently asked questions

Why does it ask for the birth time down to the minute?
Earth turns about one degree every four minutes, so the Ascendant shifts with even a small change in time. When the Ascendant moves, every house cusp moves with it and the planets fall into different houses. Sun and Moon signs barely change, but the accuracy of the house analysis depends heavily on the minute you enter.
Which time zone should I enter the birth time in?
Enter the time exactly as the clock read at the birth place. There is no need to convert to UTC. Selecting a city applies its IANA time zone automatically, and the actual UTC offset and daylight-saving state for that date are worked out for you.
What if I do not know my birth time?
Without it, the Ascendant and house analysis lose a good deal of accuracy. The ecliptic longitudes of the Sun, Moon and planets, the element and modality distribution, and the aspects between bodies all remain usable. Entering something near midday and treating the house material as indicative is the sensible approach.
How are the planetary positions calculated?
Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 computes geocentric ecliptic longitudes for the exact moment of birth. No lookup table maps a birth date to a Sun sign, and no positions are stored in advance. The Ascendant is derived from local sidereal time, latitude and the obliquity of the ecliptic at that date, giving the point where the ecliptic meets the eastern horizon.
What if I was born somewhere not on the list?
Use Custom Location. Enter the latitude, longitude and IANA time zone and it calculates exactly as a listed city would. The time zone is never guessed from coordinates, so you do need to select it yourself.
How much weight should I give the result?
The coordinates themselves come from astronomical calculation, but the reading of temperament and life areas follows the symbolic framework of Western astrology. Take it as material for understanding yourself better, and leave decisions about work or health to the situation in front of you and your own judgement.
