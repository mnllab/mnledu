The problem with taking notes on a video

You watch a lecture, write down the useful parts, and come back a week later wanting to check one of them. Now you are scrubbing through two hours of footage trying to find where it was said.

This puts the video and a notepad side by side, and one keystroke drops the current playback time into your notes. Later, that timestamp tells you exactly where to look, and clicking it jumps straight there.

When to use it

Lecture notes
Recording points as they come up, with the time attached for revision.
Conference talks
Marking the parts of a long recording worth quoting or returning to.
Summaries for a team
Notes with timestamps let colleagues verify a point without watching the whole thing.
Chapter markers
Drafting the chapter list for your own video, exportable as markdown for the description.

How to use it

  1. Paste a YouTube address and press Load video.
  2. Write in the notes panel while you watch.
  3. Press Ctrl+Enter at any point to insert the current playback time.
  4. Export as markdown or plain text.

Settings

Markdown with links
Turns each timestamp into a link that jumps to that point. Useful in Notion, GitHub or anywhere markdown renders.
Markdown, plain
Keeps the times as text without links.
Plain text
No formatting at all.

Frequently asked questions

Are my notes saved
They autosave in your browser, so they survive a reload on the same device. Nothing is sent to a server, so keep a copy of anything important.
Which addresses work
Both the short youtu.be form and the full youtube.com/watch?v= form.
Some videos will not load
Videos whose owners have disabled embedded playback cannot be loaded here. That is a restriction set by the uploader and there is no way around it.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
