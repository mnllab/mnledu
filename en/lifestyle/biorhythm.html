💌 A word for today

A biorhythm is simply the days since your birth divided into fixed cycles.
Take it as a prompt to check in with yourself, not as a rule for your day.

Why some days drag

Some days the work almost does itself; other days you push at the same task and nothing moves. It is easy to blame yourself for the second kind, but bodies and moods have always risen and fallen.

A biorhythm takes that rise and fall and assumes a fixed cycle for it. Treat it less as a forecast to be proved right or wrong, and more as a prompt to notice how you are actually doing today.

The three rhythms

🏃 Physical · 23 days
Stamina and physical drive. When it runs high, schedule the workout or the tasks that need your hands.
💖 Emotional · 28 days
Mood and how you meet other people. High suits company and conversation; low suits quiet on your own.
🧠 Intellectual · 33 days
Focus and judgement. Pull the difficult decisions and the new learning forward when this one is up.

Reading the curve

Above the line · flow
Energy is heading outward. Good for bringing out what you have been putting off — though feeling strong is not a reason to spend everything at once.
Below the line · recharge
Energy is gathering inward. Not a stretch where you cannot, but one where you refill. Sleep and food matter more here, and the next rise arrives easier for it.
Crossing zero · turning point
The day the flow changes direction. Body and mind are adjusting to a new pattern, so an ordinary pace tends to feel better than a demanding schedule.
When they disagree
Common, and no cause for concern. Lean on whichever is running high, spare the one that is resting, and the day goes much more smoothly.

How to use it

  1. Enter your date of birth and press Read my rhythms.
  2. Check where the three sit today on the gauges.
  3. Look at the fortnight either side on the chart to see where to place what matters.
  4. Pick one suggestion below that you could actually do today.
  5. Change the date to look ahead at any other day.

Frequently asked questions

How is the biorhythm worked out?
It counts the days from your birthday to the date you choose, then feeds that into sine curves of 23 days for physical, 28 for emotional and 33 for intellectual. Everything is calculated in your browser and the dates you type are never sent anywhere.
Is a dip below the line a bad day?
No. The lower stretch is when energy gathers inward — a recharging phase. This tool reads it as guidance on resting well, and treats neither direction as good or bad.
What is a turning point?
A day when the curve crosses zero and changes direction. A new rhythm is settling in, so an ordinary pace tends to feel better than launching something big — let the shift bed down.
The three rhythms disagree — which should I follow?
Lean on whichever is running high and go easy on the one that is resting. If your body feels light but your thinking is heavy, put the physical tasks first and leave the judgement calls for another day.
Can I look at past or future dates?
Yes. Change the date and you will see that day's reading, with the chart drawing two weeks either side of it. Useful for sensing the shape of a week before a trip or something that matters.

A biorhythm is not a scientifically established predictor — it is the days since your birth divided into fixed cycles. Take it as a prompt to check in with yourself rather than a basis for health or important decisions, and if your body stays heavy or your mood stays low for a long stretch, please speak to a professional.
