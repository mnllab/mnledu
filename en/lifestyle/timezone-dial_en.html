Why time zone arithmetic keeps catching people out

Learning that New York is thirteen hours behind Seoul works until summer, when it becomes fourteen. Daylight saving shifts the gap, the start dates differ by country, and between March and November the arithmetic quietly stops matching.

This tool sidesteps the calculation. Put the cities side by side and drag the dial: every clock moves together, so you read the answer instead of working it out.

When to use it

Scheduling across time zones
Checking what time it is for the other side, and whether it falls inside their working day.
Joining an overseas event
Converting a webinar or conference start time into your local time.
Planning travel
Seeing departure and arrival times together while you build an itinerary.
Calling family abroad
A quick look before you dial.

How to use it

  1. Use Add a city to bring in the places you care about.
  2. Set a reference time, or press Jump to now.
  3. Drag the dial and every clock moves with it.

Frequently asked questions

Is daylight saving handled
Each city follows its own time zone rules, including daylight saving. Rules do change occasionally, so confirm anything critical with the other party.
How should I write a time in an invitation
Give both sides, for example "3pm New York (8pm London)". Time zone abbreviations alone are ambiguous once daylight saving is in play.
Are my chosen cities remembered
They persist in your browser for the session. Nothing is sent to a server.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
