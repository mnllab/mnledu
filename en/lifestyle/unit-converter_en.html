Universal Unit Converter

Type into any field and every other one fills in at once

Converting units without a page full of ads

Inches to centimetres, pounds to kilograms, square feet to square metres. Each one is a small question, and searching for it means loading a page covered in advertising and then fighting to copy the answer.

Here every related unit sits on one screen. Type a number into any field and the rest fill in together, so you can read off whichever one you need.

When to use it

Property and floor area
Square feet to square metres when comparing listings or lease terms across markets.
Product specifications
Converting inches, pounds and Fahrenheit from an overseas datasheet.
Shipping and customs
Restating weight and volume in the units the destination country expects.
Cooking
Ounces, cups and Fahrenheit into grams and Celsius, or the other way round.

How to use it

  1. Pick a category.
  2. Type a value into any field.
  3. The equivalent values appear in every other unit. Copy whichever you need.

Settings

Decimal places
Auto, or a fixed number of places. A fixed setting reads better in a document.

Frequently asked questions

What are the units marked KR
Traditional Korean measures that still appear in property listings and markets there: pyeong for area, geun and gwan for weight, doe and mal for volume. They are included because this site started in Korea, and they are grouped at the end of each list.
Why is my drive smaller than advertised
Manufacturers count a gigabyte as 1,000,000,000 bytes while operating systems use 1,073,741,824. Data conversions here use the binary figure, which is why the numbers differ.
Is temperature supported
Yes, in Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin. Temperature scales have different zero points rather than being simple ratios, so they are calculated differently from the other categories.
