Uploading a face just to crop it never sits right

Search for a way to crop a photo or clear its background and most results ask you to upload the file to their site. They promise to delete it shortly after, but handing a picture of your own face to somebody else’s server is not a comfortable trade.

This tool sends nothing anywhere. Opening the file, cropping, rotating, resizing, removing the background and saving all finish inside your browser. Cut the connection entirely and everything except background removal still works.

What it does

✂️ Crop
Drag the corners to take exactly what you want. Lock to 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9, and use the fine angle slider to straighten a shot that came out tilted.
🔄 Rotate and flip
Ninety degrees either way, plus a horizontal flip. Useful when a photo came out sideways or you want the mirror version.
📐 Resize
Enter width and height in pixels. With the ratio locked, filling one field fills the other. Quick buttons cover 50%, 75% and 200%.
🩹 Clear white background
Makes the paper behind a signature, stamp or piece of handwriting transparent immediately, with nothing to download. Move the slider and the canvas updates live, so you can see exactly how much is being cleared.
✨ AI cutout
For portraits and anything with a busy background. Keeps the subject and clears everything behind it. The result saves as PNG, so it drops straight onto another background.
📋 Copy and save
Put the edited photo on the clipboard to paste into a document or a chat, or download it as a file.

Two ways to clear a background

Lifting a signature off a sheet of paper and cutting a person out of a photograph are not the same problem, so there are two tools rather than one.

Clearing white turns bright pixels transparent. Because the idea is that simple, it finishes the instant you press it with nothing to download. For signatures, stamps, handwriting and scanned drawings — anything where the background is pale and the subject is dark — this is both faster and cleaner.

The right brightness level differs from photo to photo, so the tool reads the border pixels and settles on a value that fits. Clean white paper lands around 240; yellowed paper or a shadowed shot drops to somewhere between 160 and 210 on its own.

Press Start and a preview appears at that level, and the canvas updates as you move the slider. It also tells you what percentage survived, so you can see at a glance whether too much or too little is going. To keep the sliders smooth on large photos the preview runs on a scaled-down copy, and Apply recalculates at full resolution.

The AI cutout works out what the subject actually is. That is what you need when the background is busy, or when subject and background sit at a similar brightness. The trade is that it downloads a model first.

How the AI cutout works here

Recognising a background takes a segmentation model. That normally runs on a server, but this tool brings the model into your browser and runs it on your own device. That is why the photo never has to leave the machine.

The trade is that the model file has to be downloaded once. Three sizes are available.

  • Small (about 11MB) — quick, with somewhat rougher edges. Good when you are in a hurry.
  • Medium (about 44MB) — the default, balancing speed against quality.
  • Large (about 88MB) — holds fine edges like hair much better, and takes longer.

It downloads once and stays cached, so later runs are far quicker. You are asked before the download starts, so you can decline when your data allowance is tight.

How to use it

  1. Open a photo — press the button, click the empty canvas, drag a file in, or paste with Ctrl+V.
  2. Use Crop to set the framing and straighten any tilt.
  3. For a signature or stamp use Clear white; for a portrait use the AI cutout.
  4. Set the pixel size you need under Resize.
  5. Copy image to paste it somewhere, or Save to download the file.

Scroll to zoom and drag to pan; double-click returns to the original scale. Ctrl+Z steps back one edit at a time. Undo edits clears your changes but keeps the photo; Start over clears the photo as well so you can open another.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No. Opening the file, cropping, rotating, removing the background and saving all happen inside your browser. Close the page and the work goes with it.
What is the difference between clearing white and the AI cutout?
Clearing white turns bright pixels transparent. Because the idea is simple it runs the moment you press it, with nothing to download, and it suits signatures, stamps and handwriting sitting on pale paper. The AI cutout works out what the subject actually is, which is what you need for portraits or busy backgrounds, and it downloads a model once before it can run.
What does automatic brightness detection do?
It reads the brightness around the edges of the photo and settles on a level that fits that particular shot, so yellowed paper or a shadow across one side is handled without any adjustment. Pressing Start shows a preview at that level, and moving a slider updates the result as you go. Press Apply once it looks right.
How does the AI cutout work?
Your browser downloads a segmentation model and runs it on your own device. The photo never leaves the machine — only the model file is fetched. The first run takes a while to download it; after that it stays cached and runs much faster.
How long does the AI cutout take?
The first run can take tens of seconds while the model downloads. Once it is cached, a single photo usually takes a few seconds to a dozen or so, depending on your device and the image size.
How is the save format decided?
If any part of the image is transparent it saves as PNG; otherwise it saves as the smaller JPG. After removing a background there are transparent areas, so the file comes out as PNG and drops straight onto another background.
Can I paste the edited photo directly?
Press Copy image and it goes to the clipboard as a picture, ready to paste into a document or a chat window. If your browser blocks clipboard images, the tool falls back to downloading the file.
How many ways can I open a photo?
Four. Use the Open image button, click the empty editing area, drag a file onto the page, or press the paste shortcut anywhere to bring in whatever picture is on your clipboard. There is a Paste button as well.
What is the difference between Undo edits and Start over?
Undo edits keeps the photo you have open and rolls back everything you did to it. Start over clears the photo too and returns to the opening screen, which is what you want before editing a different picture.

Every edit runs inside your browser and no photo is transmitted to any server. Background removal needs an internet connection to fetch the model file; even then, the photo itself is never sent. Very large photos are opened with their longest edge scaled to 4000px so that editing stays responsive.
