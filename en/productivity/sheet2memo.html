A spreadsheet is not built for skimming

Keep a task list or an enquiry log in a sheet and you end up scrolling sideways to read a single row. A long note in one column shows as a truncated sliver, and you have to click the cell and read it out of the formula bar.

This lays each row out as one sticky note. What was lying sideways stands up, and clicking a sentence copies that sentence.

How it reads your sheet

Row one = headings
Each cell in the first row becomes a field name, the label you see in front of a value like Owner: Kim.
Row two onwards = notes
One row, one note. Blank rows in between are skipped.
Column one = the title
The first column appears large at the top of the note, so put the task name or subject there.
Remaining columns = body
Short values become badges; longer text is listed underneath by field.

Long text is split into sentences

Text in a notes or comments column is broken on line breaks and full stops, one sentence per line, so you can pick out just the sentence you need.

A dot between digits is not treated as a sentence end. Values like 1.5bn or v2.1.0 stay whole rather than being cut in half.

Connecting a Google Sheet

Share the sheet as anyone with the link can view, then paste the address from your browser bar. It is converted into a download link for you, and the tab you were looking at is the one that loads.

A private sheet cannot be read by the browser. If sharing is out of the question for internal material, export it as Excel or CSV and drop that in instead — that route never leaves your machine.

How to use it

  1. Paste a sheet link, or drop an Excel file onto the page.
  2. Once the notes appear, use search to narrow them down.
  3. When a column holds a small set of repeated values, filter buttons are built automatically. Split the board by status or owner.
  4. Click a sentence to copy it, or use the button on a note to take the whole card.
  5. Edited the sheet? Press reload.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect a Google Sheet?
Share the sheet so that anyone with the link can view it, then paste the address from your browser bar. It is converted into a download link for you. A private sheet cannot be read by the browser, so change the sharing setting first.
Which row becomes the heading?
The first row. From the second row on, each row becomes one note, and blank rows in between are skipped. The first column appears large at the top of the note and the remaining columns are listed underneath by field.
How is long text displayed?
It is split into sentences on line breaks and full stops, one per line. A dot between digits, as in a decimal or a version number, is not treated as the end of a sentence, so values stay intact.
How does copying work?
Click a sentence and just that sentence is copied. The button on the note copies the whole card with its fields labelled. You can also take every note currently on screen in one go.
Are my files uploaded?
Excel files are read directly by your browser and never uploaded. With a Google Sheets link your browser fetches the content from Google, but that content does not pass through us.
What if there are too many notes?
Type in the search box and only notes containing that text remain. When a column holds a small set of repeated values, filter buttons are built from it automatically, so you can split the board by status or owner.

Excel and CSV files are read directly by your browser and never sent to any server. With a Google Sheets link your browser fetches the content from Google directly; even then it does not pass through us. Closing the page clears what was loaded, so copy or download anything you need to keep.
