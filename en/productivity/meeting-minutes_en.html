What happens when minutes get pasted around

Minutes are rarely written once and left alone. They go out by email, get pasted into a chat channel, then dropped into a report. Formatting breaks at every step — tables lose their columns in a plain editor, and line breaks scramble in a messenger.

So this writes once and exports four ways, each shaped for where it is going. Paste and move on, with no cleanup.

Who finds this useful

Q&A-driven meetings
Reviews, advisory sessions and briefings where the questions are the record.
Meetings that leave loose ends
When some questions could not be answered on the spot.
Sharing across channels
When the same notes must land in email, chat and a report.
Personal archiving
Moving notes into Notion or a Markdown repository.

How to use it

  1. Enter the title and time. The Now button fills in the date with its weekday.
  2. Record what was asked under Questions & answers, marking anything unresolved as Follow-up.
  3. Add anything that was asked of you under Requests.
  4. If another meeting is set, pick the date under Next meeting.
  5. Press the copy button matching where you are pasting.

The four export formats

📝 Plain text
Strips tables and tags, aligning columns with spaces. CJK characters take two columns to a Latin letter's one, so tab alignment falls apart — actual display width is measured instead.
📄 Word
Carries tables, bold text and colour onto the clipboard. A plain paste arrives fully formatted.
💬 Slack · chat
No tables at all, just emoji and line breaks. Readable on a narrow screen.
📓 Markdown
Headings, block quotes and checkbox syntax. Pastes into Notion as native blocks.

Frequently asked questions

Will the layout break when I paste into a plain text editor?
No. Columns are aligned with spaces based on each character's actual display width rather than tabs. CJK characters occupy two columns where Latin letters take one, and that difference is what throws tab-aligned text out of line — it is accounted for here.
Does table formatting survive a paste into Word?
Yes. The Word copy places rich content on the clipboard — tables, bold text and colours intact — so a plain paste lands fully formatted, with no cleanup needed.
Will I lose my work if the tab closes?
No. Everything is saved to your device as you type, so reopening the page restores what you had. Use the backup button to download a file if you want to carry the notes to another machine.
How are unanswered questions handled?
Mark a question as needing follow-up and it turns amber on screen. Every export also gathers those questions into a list at the end, so nothing outstanding gets buried in the body of the notes.
Is anything I type sent to a server?
No. All processing happens inside your browser and the notes stay on the device you are using.
