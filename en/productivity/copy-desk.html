Eight windows open, jumping between all of them

You refine a prompt and paste it into a chat window, edit a script draft and move it into a document, save a reference link to use again later. Each time you lose a moment working out which window has what.

A notes app means several windows; one long document means hunting for the bit you want to copy. This sits in the gap. Keep work-in-progress as cards — edit it when you need to, then pull it out with one click when you are ready to use it.

Why there are two modes

Refining text and pasting it around are different motions. Refining wants a card to open in an editor; pasting wants it copied the instant you click. Separating the two keeps both actions predictable.

✍️ Edit mode
Click a card on the right and it loads into the editor on the left. Keep writing and the card updates as you type, saving after you pause.
⚡ Quick-copy mode
Hovering shows a copy overlay; clicking puts the card on the clipboard immediately. Links copy the address, and the arrow opens them in a new tab.

Ctrl+E switches modes, Ctrl+S saves, and Ctrl+N starts a new note.

The editor is also the input box

You do not need to open a separate new-note dialog. Just start typing in the editor on the left and click Add; the note appears as a card on the shelf to the right.

Click an existing card and its contents load back into the editor. The Add button becomes Save changes, while text edits are reflected on the card as you type.

The status next to the editor title tells you whether you are drafting a new note or editing an existing one.

Titles write themselves

Leave the title blank and the first non-empty line becomes the title. Leading blank lines are skipped, and hash marks or list bullets are stripped off.

Edit that first line later and the title follows. Once you type a title by hand, it stays put.

Labels, order and batch deletion

Labelled cards get a coloured badge, and the dropdown filters the shelf to a single label. Labels you have not used do not clutter the list.

Drag a card to move it. Put the ones you reach for most at the top. The order is saved with everything else.

Use the checkbox on each card to select several notes and delete them together. An undo appears briefly after deletion.

Images are scaled down

Browser storage is not generous, so images are scaled to 800 pixels on the long edge and converted to a lighter format.

Images with transparency keep PNG, since logos often need an empty background. Current usage is shown at the bottom and you are warned as storage fills.

Copying an image card puts the image itself on the clipboard, so it can be pasted straight into a document or a deck.

If you delete something by mistake

Deleting a note, deleting a selected group, or clearing the desk leaves an undo on screen for a few seconds. Use it before it disappears to restore what you removed.

This is a place to park work in progress. Reference material you want to keep long-term belongs in a document or a dedicated tool.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the two modes?
In edit mode, clicking a card on the right loads it into the editor on the left. In quick-copy mode, clicking a card copies its contents immediately. Use Ctrl+E to switch between them.
How do I create a new note?
Start typing directly in the editor on the left and click Add. The note appears on the shelf to the right. Press Ctrl+N whenever you want a fresh editor.
What happens if I leave the title blank?
The first non-empty line becomes the title. Leading blank lines are skipped and markdown or list markers are stripped off. Once you type a title yourself, that manual title is kept.
When does my typing get saved?
While editing an existing note, the card updates as you type and browser storage is updated shortly after you pause. The page also saves before the tab closes.
How many images can I add?
Browser storage is limited, so images are scaled to 800 pixels on the long edge and compressed. Transparent images keep PNG. Current storage usage is shown at the bottom.
Can I reorder or delete several cards at once?
Drag cards to reorder them. Use the checkboxes to select several cards and delete them together; an undo appears briefly afterwards.

Everything runs inside your browser and nothing you save is sent to a server. It uses this browser's local storage, so notes do not automatically appear on other devices and may disappear if browser data is cleared or you use a private window. Treat this as a workspace for temporary material and keep long-term content somewhere more permanent.
