The trouble with signing one line

Adding a signature to a contract usually means printing it, signing it, and scanning it back in. That costs paper and time, and the result is invariably worse looking than the original.

Photograph your signature once and you can reuse it whenever it is needed. This tool strips the white background from that photo, makes it transparent, and stamps it wherever you want on a PDF.

Where it helps

Contracts and agreements
Stamp the signature line and send the file straight back.
Forms and submissions
When the same signature has to appear on several pages.
Quotes and confirmations
Turning something around without a printer or scanner.
Seals and stamps
A scanned company seal or personal stamp works exactly the same way.

How to use it

  1. Open your document with Open PDF, or drag it onto the page.
  2. Use Upload signature image on the right. The white background is removed automatically.
  3. If the result looks patchy, adjust the transparency threshold.
  4. Drag the preview onto the PDF where you want it, or press the place button.
  5. Drag the signature to fine-tune the position, and use the corner handle to resize it.
  6. Move to other pages and repeat wherever a signature is needed.
  7. Press Download signed PDF to save the merged file.

Getting a clean cut-out

Dark ink on white paper
The greater the contrast, the cleaner the result. A ballpoint or felt-tip beats a pencil.
Photograph in good light
Shadows are not read as background. Shoot near a window or under even lighting.
Transparency threshold
Lower values erase darker pixels too. Lower it if background remains, raise it if strokes vanish.
Edge softness
Raise this when the outline of the signature looks jagged or stair-stepped.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF and signature uploaded anywhere?
No. Reading the file, erasing the background, stamping the signature and saving the result all happen inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the page discards the work.
Can I just photograph a signature on paper?
Yes. Sign on white paper, photograph it in good light and upload it — the background is removed automatically. If the paper looks yellow or has a shadow, lower the transparency threshold until it clears.
Can I sign more than one page?
Yes. Move through the pages and drop a signature wherever you need one. Each page keeps its own position and size, and all of them are merged when you save.
Will the signature shift position in the saved PDF?
No. The on-screen position is converted to the PDF's own coordinate system, with the Y axis flipped, and each page's scale is calculated separately so mixed page sizes stay accurate.
The background will not come off cleanly
Lower the transparency threshold — the lower the value, the darker the pixels it will erase. If the strokes start disappearing, raise it again, and increase the edge softness if the outline looks jagged.
