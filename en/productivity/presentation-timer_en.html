Two problems every host runs into

Timing a session always brings up the same two headaches. One is losing track of whether the talk has finished and the Q&A has begun. The other is doing mental arithmetic to work out how far behind the whole programme has slipped.

This tool runs the talk and the Q&A as one continuous flow, and records the moment the host moves to the next slot so the rest of the schedule can be recalculated.

Where it helps

Seminars and academic panels
When talks and Q&A are timed separately across many speakers.
Reviews and judging
When every presenter must get the same amount of time and fairness matters.
Internal meetings and workshops
When each agenda item has a set length and you need to see the drift.
Online events
When you want the timer floating above your video call window.

How to use it

  1. Press Agenda and set the presentation length, Q&A length, and event start time.
  2. Enter one slot per line, or drop a CSV file onto the box, then press Apply agenda.
  3. Press Start. Presentation time runs first and rolls straight into Q&A when it hits zero.
  4. Press Next speaker to move on. That moment is recorded and used to work out whether you are behind.
  5. Open the mini window to keep the timer above your slides.

    6. Colours and sounds by phase

    Presenting
    Counts down in green.
    Q&A
    The moment presentation time reaches zero it carries straight on without stopping. The colour turns amber and a chime sounds once.
    Overtime
    Once Q&A is also spent the display turns red and flashes at one-second intervals. Instead of time remaining, it counts the overrun upward with a plus sign.

    Options

    Per-slot length
    Add a number at the end of a line and that slot gets its own length. Useful for short items like a break or opening remarks.
    Extend buttons
    Time is added to the phase you are currently in. During the talk it extends the talk; during Q&A it extends the Q&A.
    Column labels
    Rename columns like affiliation or topic to suit your event. For a judging panel you might use team name and project instead.
    Mini window
    A small always-on-top window. Extend, pause, and move between speakers from inside it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does it move to the next speaker automatically when time runs out?
    No. It keeps counting the overtime and shows it on screen, but the session only advances when the host presses the button. This is deliberate, so the display never moves on while someone is still speaking.
    Can I keep the timer on top of my slides?
    Press the mini window button and a small always-on-top window opens. Place it over your slides or video call, and you can extend time, pause, and move between speakers from inside that window. It works in Chromium-based browsers, and if the page is embedded in another site you need to open it in a new tab first.
    How is the delay calculated?
    It compares the clock time when the host pressed next speaker against the originally scheduled time. Later means running behind, earlier means running ahead, and the expected start time for every remaining slot is recalculated.
    What format should the agenda be in?
    One line per slot, separated by commas: session, affiliation, speaker, topic, notes. Add a number at the end of a line to give that slot its own length, which is handy for short items like a break or opening remarks. You can also drop a CSV file onto the box.
    Is my agenda sent to a server?
    No. Everything runs inside your browser, and the agenda and settings are stored only on the device you are using.
    The mini window will not open
    When the page is embedded inside another one, browser policy blocks it. A notice appears with an Open in a new tab and retry button — use it to open the page on its own. The feature is also limited to Chromium-based browsers.
    Will my agenda survive a refresh?
    Yes. What you enter is saved on your device and will still be there when you come back.
    Can I skip ahead to a specific slot?
    Press the Start button on any row of the table to jump straight to it. That moment is recorded too.
