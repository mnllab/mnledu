Why copying from a PDF breaks every line

A PDF does not store sentences the way a word processor does. It places each visible line of glyphs at a set of coordinates on the page. What looks like a flowing paragraph is, inside the file, a stack of separate lines.

So when you copy and paste, the line breaks you could see come with it. Pull a paragraph out of a paper or a report and it arrives split across four or five lines, and often with words hyphenated across the break.

When to use it

Quoting papers and reports
Moving a passage from a PDF into a document. Cleaning up the breaks by hand takes longer than the quote itself.
Feeding text to an AI model
Line breaks waste tokens and fragment the context. A clean paragraph gets a better answer.
Extracting from funding calls
Pulling eligibility criteria or funding details out of a PDF notice and into a table or a note.
Machine translation
Translation engines treat a line break as the end of a sentence. Fragmented input produces noticeably worse output.

How to use it

  1. Copy the section you want from the PDF and paste it into Input.
  2. The rebuilt paragraphs appear on the right straight away.
  3. Pick a copy format and press Copy.

Settings

Join CJK lines without a space
English needs a space between words, but Chinese, Japanese and Korean do not. With this on, CJK lines are joined directly instead of gaining a gap in the middle of a word.
Keep line breaks on list and numbered lines
Lines starting with a bullet, a number or a marker carry meaning in their line break, so they are left alone.
Copy format
Choose whether to keep a blank line between paragraphs, separate them without blank lines, or collapse everything into a single paragraph.

Frequently asked questions

Is anything uploaded
No. The text you paste is handled inside your browser and is never transmitted.
What about tables
In a table the line breaks are the data structure, so this tool is the wrong fit. Use it for body text.
Can I drop in a PDF file directly
This tool works on text you have already copied. For a scanned PDF where the text cannot be selected, use the image to text tool instead.
What happens to hyphenated words
Words broken across a line with a hyphen are rejoined, and the count of merged hyphens is shown so you can spot anything that was joined incorrectly.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
