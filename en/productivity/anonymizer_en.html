What has to come out before you share a file

Meeting notes and contact lists get shared, pasted into AI tools and attached to tickets. Before any of that, the phone numbers and email addresses have to go. Doing it by hand is slow and unreliable, and in a long document you will miss some.

This tool finds personal details by their shape rather than their meaning. Eleven digit mobile numbers, strings containing an at sign, national ID and company registration patterns, and IP addresses are all recognised and masked.

When to use it

Before pasting into an AI tool
Support transcripts and contact lists are much safer to hand over once the identifiers are gone.
Sharing meeting notes
Notes taken during a call often carry phone numbers in the body text.
Turning real work into examples
Using an actual document as a template or a teaching sample. The structure stays, the identities go.
Filing bug reports
Screenshots are awkward to redact. Text goes through here in one pass.

How to use it

  1. Paste the text into the left panel.
  2. Switch on the categories you want masked.
  3. Read through the result, then copy or save it.

Settings

Phone numbers
Matches mobile and landline formats, including international prefixes.
Email addresses
Anything shaped like an address containing an at sign.
ID and company numbers
National identification and business registration number patterns.
IP addresses
Both private and public addresses, which often appear in logs and technical notes.

Frequently asked questions

Does it mask names
No. A name has no distinguishing format, so it cannot be detected reliably. Read the result and edit any names yourself.
Can I trust it to catch everything
Details written in an unusual way, such as a number with spaces inside it, can slip through. Always read the result before you share it. This tool reduces the work, it does not replace the check.
Are amounts and dates affected
No. Figures like 1,234-5678 and dates like 2026-08-27 are deliberately left alone, since they look similar to some ID formats but are not personal data.
Can I undo the masking
Not from the result, but your original stays untouched in the left panel for as long as the page is open.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
