How to use

  • Pick a tool from the toolbar: pen, eraser, line, rectangle, circle, fill, text, or eyedropper.
  • Click a color swatch or use the custom color button to choose any color.
  • Use the size slider to adjust line thickness — the circle next to it previews the actual size, and a matching circle cursor follows your pointer on the canvas.
  • Rectangle and circle tools are drawn by clicking and dragging, and can be outlined or filled.
  • The fill tool fills the clicked area and any connected area of the same color.
  • The text tool opens an input box where you click the canvas — set the font size and bold option first, type your text, then click elsewhere to apply it.
  • The eyedropper tool sets the current color to whatever you click on the canvas.
  • Undo and redo support the most recent 25 steps.
  • Save your finished drawing as PNG or JPG, or copy it straight to your clipboard to paste elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is my drawing sent to a server?
A. No. Everything happens in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
Q. What file formats can I save as?
A. PNG, JPG, and clipboard copy are all supported.
Q. What if clipboard copy doesn't work?
A. Some browsers or environments restrict clipboard access. In that case, use the Save PNG button instead.
