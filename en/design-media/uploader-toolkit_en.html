Retyping the same things every week

Every episode you edit the date in the title, recall the filename convention, and paste the greeting, the gear list and the business email into the description again. Fifteen minutes gone each time, and occasionally last week\u2019s date goes out untouched.

This reduces that to one step. Set the rules once and just pick the date.

Date variables

Put a name in square brackets into your title or filename and it resolves against the date you picked.

VariableMeaninge.g. 2026-08-30
[YYYY]four-digit year2026
[YY]two-digit year26
[MM] / [M]month08 / 8
[DD] / [D]day30 / 30
[W]week of the month5
[WW]week of the year35
[DAY]weekdaySun
[Q]quarter3

Write [YY][MM][DD]_weekly_w[W].mp4 and you get 260830_weekly_w5.mp4.

Why three ways to count weeks

The same date lands in different weeks depending on the method. 30 August 2026 is week 5 by calendar rows but week 6 counting from Sunday.

  • Calendar row, Monday start — the default. Matches the row you would count by eye.
  • Calendar row, Sunday start — for calendars that begin the week on Sunday.
  • Simple — days 1–7 are week 1, 8–14 week 2. Independent of weekday, so it is easy to predict.

Neither is more correct; match whatever your channel already uses. Switching updates the result immediately, so try them side by side.

Building the description from blocks

Save the things you reuse — greeting, timestamps, gear list, social links, business email — as blocks, then tick the ones this video needs.

Your summary for this episode goes at the top, with the ticked blocks following in order. Arrows move them around.

Blocks can contain date variables too, so anything where only the date changes is a one-time setup.

What shows before the fold

Only about the first three lines of a description are visible before the reader expands it. The preview marks that boundary with a line, so keep important links and notices above it.

The total limit is 5,000 characters. The current count is shown and you are warned if you go over.

Saved in this browser

Templates and blocks save on every edit, so they survive closing the tab. They will not appear on another device, though, and clearing browser storage removes them.

For anything you want to keep, use export settings to download a file. Import it on another machine and everything comes across.

Frequently asked questions

How is the week number calculated?
There are three options. The default matches the row you would count on a calendar starting Monday; there is also a Sunday-start version and a simple one that divides the day by seven. The same date gives different answers under each, so set it to whatever your channel already uses.
Which variables are available?
Year, month and day in two or four digits, plus week number, weekday and quarter. The variable list shows what each one resolves to for the date you picked, and clicking one inserts it into the field you were last editing.
Will my saved settings persist?
Templates and text blocks save to this browser on every edit, so closing the tab or refreshing leaves them intact. They will not appear on another device and are lost if you clear browser storage, so use the export button to keep a file.
Can I use variables in the description too?
Yes. Put them anywhere in a text block or in the summary and they resolve against the date you picked. For a recurring series where only the date changes, set it up once and just pick the date each week.
What happens to characters filenames cannot use?
Slashes, question marks and the like are replaced with underscores. Titles can use them freely, which is why keeping separate rules for the title and the filename is convenient.
Is there a length limit on the description?
Five thousand characters. The current count is shown and you are warned when it is exceeded. Only about the first three lines show before the reader expands it, so put the important things near the top.

Everything runs inside your browser and nothing you type is sent to any server. Saved settings use this browser\u2019s storage, so they do not appear on other devices and disappear if that storage is cleared. Export anything important to a file.
