Privacy Policy

What this site does and does not collect, and how advertising cookies are used.

1. The short version

Every tool on TOOLKIT runs inside your own browser. Documents, files, numbers and text you enter are not transmitted to any server operated by this site, and are not stored anywhere. Closing the page discards the working data.

There is no registration, so no name, contact details or address are collected directly.

2. What is stored in your browser

For convenience, the following is kept in your own browser storage. The operator cannot read it.

  • Favourites — the identifiers of tools you have starred
  • Recent settings in some tools — so a tool reopens the way you left it
  • Visit flags — to count visitors per tool without inflating the number on refresh or repeat visits, one flag per tool marks that this browser has already been counted.

Clearing your browsing data and site data removes this as well.

3. Server logs

The site is served through GitHub Pages. As part of delivering a website, the hosting provider may record technical information such as IP address, browser type and request time. This is for service delivery and security, and is not collected or retained by the operator.

To show roughly how many visitors each tool has had, the first time you open a tool page in a given browser it sends one anonymous signal to an external counting service. Repeat visits from the same browser do not send it again, thanks to the visit flag above, so refreshing or reopening a page does not inflate the number. The signal only carries a value identifying which tool it is, and contains no information that could identify you. The tallied number is shown as a visitor count next to each tool on the homepage.

4. Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertising through Google AdSense and other third party ad services.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Advertising cookies are operated by the advertising provider. The operator has no access to their contents, and nothing you enter into a tool is passed to any advertiser.

Refusing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Refusing cookies does not affect the tools themselves, though the favourites feature may stop working.

  • Chrome : Settings → Privacy and security → Third party cookies
  • Safari : Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge : Settings → Cookies and site permissions

5. External links

This site links to external sites such as YouTube. Their own policies apply once you follow a link, and the operator is not responsible for how they handle your data.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 14, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your rights

Because no personal data is held by the operator, there is no separate access, correction or deletion process. Information stored in your browser can be removed by you at any time. For questions about data handling, use the address below.
