About

Why a collection of browser tools with no install, no login and no payment.

What TOOLKIT is

TOOLKIT is a collection of small web tools for the jobs that interrupt other work. Cleaning the line breaks out of text copied from a PDF. Working out a growth rate. Merging a few pages of a document. Converting a unit. Each one takes a couple of minutes, but the search-and-upload cycle around it takes longer than the task itself.

The goal is to remove that cycle. Open the page and the tool is running. Close it when you are done. No account to create, no usage counter, no upgrade prompt.

How it is run

Everything happens on your device

All the tools here are client side. What you type or drop in is processed inside your browser and never sent to a server. For anything you would rather not upload to an unfamiliar site, that is the whole point: there is no route out. Close the page and the working data goes with it.

Free, and staying that way

Every tool is free. There is no payment step and no usage limit, and no paid tier is planned. Running costs are covered by advertising and by support on the YouTube channel.

Small on purpose

Each tool does one thing. Adding features until a tool becomes heavy defeats the reason it exists, which is to finish a small job quickly and get back to work.

A note on region

This site started in Korea, and a few tools are built around Korean rules that do not translate. Those are labelled Korea-specific in their description so you know before you open them. Anything that varies by country, such as the tax rate in the margin calculator, is a setting you can change rather than a fixed assumption.

Who builds it

Built and maintained by Janggil Kim. My background is in precision engineering, and my work involves technology valuation and startup education, which means a lot of documents and spreadsheets. These started as tools I needed myself and then collected in one place.

I also run a YouTube channel, MNL, about starting and running a business.

Requests and bug reports

If some part of your work is tedious, tell me and I will look at whether it can run in a browser. If a tool is broken, that is worth knowing too. Both go to the contact page or the YouTube channel comments.

Support

If a tool saved you time, you can support the channel through Super Thanks or membership, and that funding goes into building the next one. Every feature stays open either way.
