When a tool is not working

These details make it much easier to find the cause.

  • What you entered when it went wrong
  • What you expected and what actually happened
  • Your browser and device, for example Chrome on Windows or Safari on iPhone

Because everything runs in your browser, no error log reaches me. If you do not tell me, I have no way of knowing a tool broke at all.
