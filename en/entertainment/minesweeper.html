What the numbers are telling you

Open a square and a number appears. It counts the mines among the eight squares touching it. That single piece of information, repeated across the board, is enough to work out where every mine is.

It is a deduction game rather than a guessing one, although the opening moves are necessarily blind until enough numbers are exposed to reason from.

When to use it

A change of mental gear
Assembling clues into a conclusion is a different kind of thinking from most work.
Short rounds
A beginner board is over quickly, which suits a brief gap.

How to use it

  1. Choose a difficulty and press Start.
  2. Tap a square to open it and read the numbers.
  3. Flag the squares you believe hold mines.
  4. Open every safe square to win.

Frequently asked questions

Can I lose on the first click
No. The first square you open and its neighbours are always clear, so the board is generated around your opening move.
Are my times saved
Best times per difficulty are kept in your browser and can be cleared in the settings.
What does tapping a number do
If you have flagged as many mines around it as the number says, tapping it opens all the remaining neighbours at once. It speeds things up considerably, and misplaces flags cost you.
