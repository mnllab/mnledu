R E A D I N G

Cards as a prompt rather than a prediction

Tarot is more useful as a way of turning something over than as a forecast. Reading what a card is said to mean nudges you into looking at your situation from an angle you had not tried.

This deck draws either a single card for the day or three for past, present and future. Cards come up at random, and it is meant to be taken lightly.

When to use it

Starting the day
One card in the morning as a thought to carry around.
Working something out
A different angle on a question that has been going in circles.
With other people
Taking turns to draw and talk about what comes up.

How to use it

  1. Choose a spread: one card or three.
  2. Draw.
  3. Press Read them for the interpretation.

Settings

Today's card
A single card as a theme for the day.
Past, present, future
Three cards read as a sequence.
Shuffle again
Reshuffles and draws afresh.

Frequently asked questions

Are the results fixed
No, cards are drawn at random. The same question will give you something different each time.
Should I use this for real decisions
Please take it as entertainment. Base actual decisions on facts and evidence.
Which deck is this
The 22 major arcana of the Rider-Waite-Smith deck, whose imagery is in the public domain.
