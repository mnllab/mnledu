Picking numbers, without picking them yourself

Choosing lottery numbers by hand means thinking about it every week. Letting the terminal quick pick means you had no say at all. This tool sits between the two: keep the numbers that matter to you, exclude the ones you do not want, and let the rest be random.

Powerball and Mega Millions are configured with their official rules — five main numbers from 1 to 69 plus a Powerball from 1 to 26, and five from 1 to 70 plus a Mega Ball from 1 to 25. Korea Lotto 6/45 is included as well.

When to use it

A quick pick you still control
Get five sets at once without thinking about it, while keeping any numbers you always play.
Keeping meaningful numbers
Birthdays or anniversaries stay in every set while the remaining slots are filled at random.
Excluding numbers
Leave out numbers you would rather not see, for whatever reason.
Keeping a record
Save the sets as text so you can check them after the draw.

How to use it

  1. Choose Powerball, Mega Millions or Korea Lotto at the top.
  2. Enter any lucky numbers you want kept, or leave the fields blank for a fully automatic draw.
  3. Add any numbers to exclude.
  4. Draw, then use Copy all numbers or Save as text.

Settings

How lucky numbers are used
Choose whether one set is drawn with none of your numbers, or every set includes at least one of them. Mixing both spreads your picks across the five sets.
Excluded numbers
Numbers listed here never appear in any set.
Seed
Each draw shows the seed it used, so an identical seed reproduces the same numbers. It is there so you can verify the draw was not adjusted afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Does this improve my odds
No. Every combination has exactly the same probability however it was chosen. This tool only saves you the effort of picking.
Does it analyse past winning numbers
No. Each draw is independent, so previous results have no bearing on the next one. Patterns in past numbers are coincidence, not signal.
Why is Powerball the default
It is the most widely played of the three. You can switch to Mega Millions or Korea Lotto at the top and the rules change accordingly.
Are my numbers stored anywhere
No. Everything happens in your browser. Saving as text writes a file to your own device and nothing is sent to a server.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
