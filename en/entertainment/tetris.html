Stacking blocks in a browser tab

Rotate the falling pieces, fill a horizontal row, and the row clears. The rules take five seconds to learn, but the speed rises as you go and the thinking time shrinks with it.

No install and no sign in. It is here for the few minutes when you need to look at something else.

When to use it

A short break
A few minutes away when concentration has gone.
Waiting for something
Filling the gap while a build runs or a meeting starts.
On a phone
The same page works on mobile, with optional haptic feedback.

How to use it

  1. Press New game.
  2. Move and rotate the falling piece to fill a row.
  3. Complete a horizontal line and it clears, scoring points.

Settings

Ghost piece
Shows where the current piece will land. Worth leaving on while you are getting used to it.
Starting level
Begin at a higher speed if the early levels are too slow.
Block theme
Neon, classic or mono.
Sound and vibration
Audio feedback, and haptics on mobile when a piece lands.

Frequently asked questions

Is my score saved
Your best score is kept in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can clear it in the settings.
Does it work on a phone
Yes. Use the on-screen buttons or swipe.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
