One rule, and everything follows from it

Every row, every column and every three by three box contains the digits one to nine exactly once. That is the whole game. The interest lies in working out where to start.

Difficulty is set by how many numbers you begin with. Easy fills in almost sequentially; expert requires holding several cells in mind at the same time.

When to use it

Quiet concentration
Something to focus on with no sound and no time pressure.
Commuting
Stop and resume without losing your place.
Winding down
A dim screen and low stimulation, which makes it a reasonable last thing of the day.

How to use it

  1. Choose a difficulty and press New puzzle.
  2. Select an empty cell and enter a number.
  3. Fill the grid so every row, column and box holds one to nine.

Settings

Difficulty
Easy through expert, which changes how many numbers you start with.
Check for mistakes as I go
Flags a number that breaks the rules immediately. Turning it off makes the puzzle considerably harder.
Highlighting
Lights up the related row, column and box, or every instance of the selected number, to make scanning easier.
Three mistakes and the game ends
Adds pressure if you want it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I lose progress if I stop
No. Resume picks up where you left off, since the state is kept in your browser.
Is every puzzle solvable
Yes, only solvable puzzles are generated. If you are stuck, there is a route you have not spotted yet.
What is the notes mode for
It lets you pencil in the candidates for a cell rather than committing to one. Essential at the harder levels.

This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere, and closing the page discards the working data.
